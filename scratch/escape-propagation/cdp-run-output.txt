=== Q1 stopPropagation vs stopImmediatePropagation ===
Q1a 同元素3个监听器，第1个调 stopPropagation → 实际被调用的: ["A","B","C"]  （期望 [A,B,C] = 同元素其他监听器**照跑**）
Q1b 同元素3个监听器，第1个调 stopImmediatePropagation → 实际被调用的: ["A"]  （期望 [A] = 同元素其他监听器**全部不跑**）
Q1c 同元素 capture 监听器调 stopPropagation → 实际: ["capture-1"]  （capture 与 bubble 是**两个不同阶段**，同元素也拦不住）

=== Q2 同节点同阶段的调用顺序 ===
Q2a document/同元素 bubble 按注册顺序 reg1,reg2,reg3 → 实际: ["reg1","reg2","reg3"]
Q2b 同元素混合注册：bubble先注册、capture后注册 → 实际: ["capture-reg2","bubble-reg1","bubble-reg3"]  （capture 先跑，**与注册顺序无关**；同阶段内才按注册顺序）
Q2c 整条路径 window/doc 各挂 capture+bubble → 实际: ["window-capture","document-capture","document-bubble","window-bubble"]

=== Q3 事件路径是否包含 window ===
Q3a composedPath()（window capture 里读）: ["#target","body","html","document","window"]
Q3b window-capture 是否先于 document-capture 跑 → 实际顺序: ["window-capture","document-capture"]

=== Q4 window capture 调 stopPropagation 后 document capture ===
Q4a window capture 里 stopPropagation 后，实际被调用的: ["window-capture"]  （期望只剩 [window-capture]）
